Privacy Policy

Wanderlands: Walking Game · effective 30 September 2026

The short version

Wanderlands does not collect, store or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

Health and motion data

With your permission, Wanderlands reads your step count and walking distance from Apple Health, or counts steps with your phone's motion sensor, to move your traveler along the map. This data is processed on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising or sold. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness; the app then falls back to walks you add by hand.

What is stored on your device

Your journey progress, chapters, choices, road-game results, settings and daily step totals are stored locally in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data under Apple's terms.

Purchases

Wanderlands Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and provides us only with anonymous purchase information needed to unlock the content on your device. We never see your name, address or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is involved and nothing is transmitted.

Children

Wanderlands does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data (for example optional cloud backup), this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first, and the app will ask for your consent.
