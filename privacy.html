Privacy Policy

Wanderlands: Walking Game · effective 30 September 2026

The short version

Wanderlands has no account. Your steps, your story and your choices stay on your phone. The app does share a small amount of anonymous data with three services: RevenueCat (to unlock Wanderlands Plus after a purchase), Google Analytics for Firebase and the Meta SDK (to measure whether the ads we run bring new walkers). Nothing we collect can name you, and we never sell data.

Health and motion data

With your permission, Wanderlands reads your step count and walking distance from Apple Health, or counts steps with your phone's motion sensor, to move your traveler along the map. This data is processed on your device only. It is never sent to us or to any of the services below, never used for advertising and never sold. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness; the app then falls back to walks you add by hand.

What is stored on your device

Your journey progress, chapters, choices, road-game results, settings and daily step totals are stored locally in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data under Apple's terms.

What is collected, and why

Purchases. Wanderlands Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, address or payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription service, receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID so that Plus stays unlocked and can be restored on a new phone. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Usage and advertising measurement. To learn which ads bring people to the app, Wanderlands uses Google Analytics for Firebase and the Meta (Facebook) SDK. They receive a device identifier, coarse usage events (for example that onboarding finished, a chapter was reached or a purchase was made, with its price) and advertising data used to attribute installs to campaigns. On iOS the app first asks your permission to track through Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt; if you decline, Meta receives no identifier that can be linked to you, and Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork reporting is used instead. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Meta's policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy.

You can change the tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is involved and nothing is transmitted.

Your rights

Because the app holds no account, there is nothing for us to look up by name. To have the anonymous identifiers above deleted from RevenueCat, Google or Meta, delete the app and reset your device's advertising identifier, or write to us and we will pass the request on. If you are in the EU, UK or California, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use; contact us at the address below.

Children

Wanderlands does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data (for example optional cloud backup), this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first, and the app will ask for your consent.
